# Fictional sourcing example

All organizations, hotels, offers and confirmations below are invented. No RFP was sent.

## Hotel-facing RFP

**Event:** Harbor View Annual Meeting  
**Location:** Charlotte, North Carolina  
**Arrival Date:** Saturday, March 13, 2027  
**Departure Date:** Monday, March 15, 2027  
**Proposal Deadline:** February 1, 2027

### Guest rooms

- **Saturday, March 13:** 20 guest rooms
- **Sunday, March 14:** 20 guest rooms and one suite
- **Total:** 41 room nights

### Meeting space

- **Date:** Sunday, March 14, 2027
- **Attendees:** 40
- **Time:** 9 a.m.–5 p.m.
- **Setup:** U-shape preferred; boardroom acceptable
- **AV and Wi-Fi:** Projector, screen and Wi-Fi requested
- **Food and beverage:** Not requested; exclude catering from this quote

### Please include in your proposal

- Availability and guest-room rates
- Meeting-space rental
- Mandatory fees, taxes and service charges
- Concessions and commission terms
- Cancellation and attrition terms
- Offer expiration date

Please send your proposal to the planner by the deadline above.
